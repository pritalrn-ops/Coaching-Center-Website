Frequently Asked Questions

Quick Answers to Common Queries

What are the admission requirements?

Students from Class 8 to 12th pass (including droppers) can enroll. We conduct a simple diagnostic test to understand your current level and recommend the best batch for you. No specific eligibility criteria - just dedication to learn!

What is the fee structure?

Our fee structure varies based on the course, duration, and batch type. We offer flexible payment plans including monthly, quarterly, and annual options. Please contact us or visit our center for detailed fee information. We also provide scholarships for deserving students.

Do you offer demo classes?

Yes! We offer free demo classes so students can experience our teaching methodology and interact with faculty before enrollment. You can book a demo class by calling us or filling the contact form above.

What is the batch size?

We maintain small batch sizes of 25-30 students for JEE and NEET courses to ensure personalized attention. For board exam preparation, batch sizes are slightly larger (30-40) but still manageable for effective learning.

What study materials do you provide?

We provide comprehensive study materials including printed modules, practice worksheets, previous year question papers, and digital resources. All materials are designed by our expert faculty and regularly updated to match current exam patterns.

What is your refund policy?

We have a transparent refund policy. If you wish to discontinue within the first month, we provide a pro-rated refund after deducting registration and material costs. For detailed refund terms and conditions, please refer to the admission form or contact our office.

How do you track student progress?

We conduct weekly tests, monthly assessments, and full-length mock exams. Detailed performance reports are shared with parents. We also have regular parent-teacher meetings to discuss student progress and areas of improvement.

Can I join mid-session?

Yes, you can join mid-session. We provide special bridge classes to help you cover missed topics. Our faculty will assess your current level and create a personalized catch-up plan to bring you up to speed with the batch.
